### 1. Purpose of the Event

- Explore real-world responsibilities of Data Analytics Engineers and DevOps Engineers in enterprise environments.
- Learn global working standards and corporate culture within Multi-National Corporations (MNCs).
- Understand career growth pathways in the AWS Cloud ecosystem and engage with AWS Community networks.
- Analyze scalable system design principles through a URL Shortening Service case study on AWS.

---

### 3. Highlighted Topics

#### Topic 1: Real-world Stories & Corporate Culture at MNCs (Mr. Dat & Mr. Cuong)
- Root Cause Analysis and strategic business proposals in Data Analytics Engineering.
- Essential professional skills: Critical Thinking, Effective Communication, Data Storytelling, Problem Solving.
- 5-Level Professional Growth Pathway: *Follower → Learner → Problem Solver → System Thinker → Super Star*.
- MNC Corporate Culture: *No-Blame Culture*, *Caring & Inclusive Environment*, and Continuous Improvement (*Kaizen*).

#### Topic 2: What Does a DevOps Engineer Really Do? (Mr. Hoang Trong)
- Essence of DevOps: Bridging Development and Operations to optimize the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
- Foundational Skills: Linux Administration, Networking, Git, CI/CD, Containerization (Docker/Kubernetes), Scripting (Python/Golang).
- Application Lifecycle Management: Build, Test, Deploy, Logging, Configuration, Environment Variables.
- DevOps Mindset: Asking *"Why"* before *"How"*, automating repetitive tasks, and leveraging AI intelligently.

#### Topic 3: From First Cloud AI Journey to AWS Partner (Mr. Hieu Nghi)
- Career Progression: From student cloud learner to Solutions Architect at an AWS Partner.
- Community Programs: Leveraging AWS Student Builder and AWS Community Builder to grow personal branding.
- Career Pathways: Key skill requirements for Solutions Architect, DevOps Engineer, and Platform Engineer roles.

#### Topic 4: A Scalable URL Shortening Service on AWS (Mr. Kien & Mr. Tho)
- System Architecture: High-throughput, sub-10ms latency, and 99.99% availability.
- AWS Service Selection:
  - *Amazon API Gateway*: API Routing & Throttling.
  - *AWS Lambda*: Base62 Link Encoding & Shortening Logic.
  - *Amazon DynamoDB*: High-speed Key-Value storage (`shortKey` -> `originalUrl`).
  - *Amazon ElastiCache (Redis)*: In-memory Caching for hot URLs.
  - *Amazon CloudFront*: Global CDN Content Delivery for instant redirects.

---

### 4. Key Takeaways

#### Technical & Architectural Thinking
- Systems Thinking for holistic cloud application design.
- High-throughput architectural principles: Decoupling, Caching, Asynchronous Processing, and NoSQL Partitioning.

#### Soft Skills & Career Guidance
- Deep understanding of No-Blame culture and root-cause analytical mindset.
- Structured career development plan for AWS certifications.
